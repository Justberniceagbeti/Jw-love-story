Our Story

From “What If???” to Forever

A random introduction. An unexpected first date. A lot of what-ifs. And one very persistent man.

01 — The Beginning

It started with a simple introduction.

Our story began in Lagos, shortly after I moved to the city for NYSC. A mutual friend reached out one day and said, “A friend of mine who is also a fashion designer wants your contact.” Being new to Lagos and open to meeting new people, I agreed.

That friend turned out to be Williams.

02 — The First Date

And somehow, we just clicked.

He sent me a message, we started talking and somehow, we just clicked. We enjoyed each other’s company almost instantly and decided to go on our first date two weeks later.

Well… Williams clearly couldn’t wait two weeks. 😂

03 — The Unexpected Date

“I’m around. Where can we meet?”

The following week, Williams called one morning and casually announced that he was coming to see me. I thought he was joking until he called again to say, “I’m around. Where can we meet?”

I was stunned. 😂

04 — What If?

“What if it works?”

Two weeks after meeting him, I agreed to date him with one condition: we had to take things slow.

Because honestly, agreeing to date someone after just two weeks was one of the wildest things I had ever done.

I had so many “what ifs” running through my mind. But eventually, I chose to ask myself a different question:

“What if it works?”

05 — Three Years & Seven Months

And it did.

It took me months to fully let myself into the relationship. It took even longer for me to finally say “I love you” back.

But through it all, Williams kept showing up, loving me and somehow finding new ways to sweep me off my feet.

Then, 3 years and 7 months later, he asked me to marry him.

06 — The Proposal

And of course… he had to do it on my birthday eve. ❤️

I had planned a worship and prayer night with my girls and thought they were all coming over. Meanwhile, Williams had an entirely different plan.

He told me he wanted to take me out for dinner. I got dressed, left my girls at home, and went to meet him.

07 — She Said Yes

Then he got down on one knee.

Before I knew what was happening, he was down on one knee. Then our friends came out.

And just like that…

He popped the question. 💍

The girl who once wondered, “You and who?” was now saying YES to forever with him.

From What If to Forever

“Look at God.”

What started with a random introduction, an unexpected first date, a lot of “what ifs,” and one very persistent man has brought us here.

From “What if it works?” to “Look at God.”

And now, we’re getting married. 🤍
