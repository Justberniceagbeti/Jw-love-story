Our Story

From “What If” to Forever

A random introduction. An unexpected first date. A lot of what-ifs. And one very persistent man.

01 — The Beginning

It started with a simple introduction.

Our story began in Lagos, shortly after I moved to the city for NYSC.

A mutual friend reached out to me one day and said, “A friend of mine who is also a fashion designer wants your contact.” Being new to Lagos and open to meeting new people, I agreed.

That friend turned out to be Williams.

He sent me a message, we started talking, and somehow, we just clicked.

02 — The First Date

And somehow, we just clicked.

I was also excited to find out that he was a fashion designer. We enjoyed each other’s company almost instantly and decided to go on our first date two weeks later.

Well… Williams clearly couldn’t wait two weeks. 😂

The following week, he called one morning and casually announced that he was coming to see me. I thought he was joking until he called again to say,

“I’m around. Where can we meet?”

I was stunned. 😂

03 — The Unexpected Date

And then he caught me. 😂

I gave him a location and about an hour later, I arrived. But instead of confidently walking into the restaurant, I stood outside peeping in to find him.

And then he caught me. 😂

My first thought?

“Ahn ahn, she didn’t tell me he’s fine na!” 🤣

We eventually had that date and surprisingly, I felt incredibly comfortable with him. That was rare for me.

We still went on our actual planned first date the following week, but by then, something had already begun.

04 — What If?

“What if it works?”

Not long after we met, Williams started telling me he wanted a wife, not a knife.

In my head, I was like, “You and who???” 😂

I tried everything to avoid him. I limited our conversations and kept my distance, but somehow, the more I pushed him away, the closer he came.

Two weeks after meeting him, I agreed to be his girlfriend, with one condition: we had to take things slow.

Because honestly, agreeing to date someone after just two weeks was one of the wildest things I had ever done. I had so many “what ifs” running through my mind.

But eventually, I chose to ask myself a different question:

“What if it works?”

And it did.

05 — He Stayed

His love was enough for both of us.

When I think about us now, all I see is God leading us to each other. At the time, I didn’t understand why everything was happening so quickly, but God knew exactly how much we needed Us.

He led us to each other when we were least expecting it and in the way we least expected.

It took me months to fully let myself into the relationship. It took even longer for me to finally say “I love you” back.

I often wondered why Williams didn’t give up on me, especially because I stressed him so much in the early stages of our relationship. I was still struggling to believe that we were truly together.

But he stayed.

He was there, loving me patiently and consistently. His love was enough for both of us. From the very first day he met me, Williams knew what he wanted and he chose to remain committed to it.

Through it all, he kept showing up, loving me and somehow finding new ways to sweep me off my feet.

06 — Three Years & Seven Months

Then, 3 years and 7 months later…

Then, 3 years and 7 months later, he asked me to marry him.

And of course, he had to do it on my birthday eve. ❤️

I had planned a worship and prayer night with my girls and thought they were all coming over. Meanwhile, Williams had an entirely different plan.

He told me he wanted to take me out for dinner. I got dressed, left my girls at home, and we went out.

Before I knew what was happening, he was down on one knee.

Then our friends came out.

And just like that…

He popped the question. 💍

07 — She Said Yes

The girl who once wondered, “You and who?”

The girl who once wondered, “You and who?” was now saying YES to forever with him.

What started with a random introduction, an unexpected first date, a lot of “what ifs,” and one very persistent man has brought us here.

God led us here and God has kept us steady.

08 — From What If to Forever

“Look at God.”

From “What if it works?” to “Look at God.”

And now, we’re getting married. 💃💃💃
